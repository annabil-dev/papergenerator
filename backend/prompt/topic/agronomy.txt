================================================================================
TOPIC GUIDE — Agronomy (Crop Science)
================================================================================
FIELD: Agricultural and Biological Sciences / Agriculture
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: APA
SUBDISCIPLINE: Agronomi Tanaman Lapang, Ilmu Tanah, Irigasi, Mekanisasi Pertanian

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. Typically reports: crop species, treatments applied (fertilizer, irrigation,
  variety), experimental design (RCBD/factorial), key results (yield kg/ha, growth parameters),
  and statistical significance.
  Example: "Yield of rice (Oryza sativa L.) increased by 23% with NPK fertilization at 200 kg/ha..."

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Importance of crop in food security / global production context.
  - Current productivity gap or agronomic problem (soil degradation, water scarcity, yield stagnation).
  - Summary of existing approaches and their limitations.
  - Research objectives (3–4 specific objectives).
  - Hypothesis: "Increasing N application will linearly increase yield up to X kg/ha."
  Subsections: Background, Problem Statement, Research Objectives, Hypothesis.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Review of Related Literature"
  - Review crop physiology related to the problem (photosynthesis, nutrient uptake).
  - Soil science background: nutrient availability, pH effects, organic matter.
  - Previous fertilization/irrigation/variety trials from similar agroecosystems.
  - Cite major references: FAO reports, IRRI publications, agricultural journals.
  - Statistical design background: ANOVA, RCBD, LSD/DMRT post-hoc tests.
  Key topics: nutrient use efficiency, water use efficiency, genetic potential of varieties.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Materials and Methods" / "Methodology"
  - Location: experimental field/station with soil characteristics (soil type, texture, pH, organic C).
  - Experimental design: Randomized Complete Block Design (RCBD), 3 replications; Factorial design (2 factors × 4 levels).
  - Treatments: fertilizer doses (kg/ha), irrigation regimes, variety comparisons.
  - Agronomic practices: planting date, spacing, pest management (standard).
  - Observations/Variables: plant height (cm), leaf area index, tillers, days to flowering, yield (kg/ha), 1000-grain weight (g), harvest index.
  - Statistical analysis: ANOVA at 5%, LSD or DMRT 5%, correlation/regression.
  - Equipment: tensiometer (soil moisture), chlorophyll meter (SPAD), digital scale.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  Section name: "Results and Discussion"
  - ANOVA table: treatment | mean ± SD | significance (a, b, c grouping).
  - Typical yield ranges: rice 3–8 ton/ha; maize 5–12 ton/ha; soybean 1.5–4 ton/ha.
  - Typical growth parameters: plant height 80–120 cm (rice); leaf area index 4–7.
  - Treatment differences: 15–40% increase in yield between control and best treatment.
  - Correlation: r = 0.78 between N uptake and yield.
  - Comparison with literature: "Results agree with [Author, Year] who found..."
  Tables: ANOVA table, treatment means with DMRT grouping, growth parameter summary.
  Figures: bar chart of yield by treatment, growth curve, soil nutrient level chart.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Bar chart: yield (ton/ha) by fertilizer treatment with error bars.
  - Line graph: growth parameters (plant height) over time (days after planting).
  - Scatter plot: correlation between N uptake and grain yield.
  - Field layout diagram (RCBD block design).

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - ANOVA table (F-value, significance for all variables).
  - Mean comparison table with DMRT grouping (a,b,c notation).
  - Soil analysis results (pH, N-total, P-available, K-exchangeable).
  - Agronomic parameters summary (mean ± SE for each treatment).

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - States which treatment gives optimal results (e.g., "NPK 200 kg/ha gives highest yield of 7.2 ton/ha").
  - Recommends practical application for farmers.
  - Limitations: single location/season; need for multi-location validation.
  - Future research: farmer field trials, economic analysis (B/C ratio).

ADDITIONAL SECTIONS:
  - "Site Description": climate data (rainfall mm, temperature °C), soil map.
  - "Economic Analysis": input cost vs. yield gain (B/C ratio).

COMMON REFERENCE VENUES:
  Field Crops Research, Plant and Soil, Agronomy Journal, Crop Science,
  European Journal of Agronomy, Soil and Tillage Research, Pedosphere,
  Indonesian Journal of Agronomy, Journal of Agricultural Science

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  Oryza sativa, Zea mays, Glycine max, NPK, urea, pH, organic matter, N-total,
  P-available, K-exchangeable, yield, harvest index, leaf area index, SPAD,
  RCBD, DMRT, LSD, agronomic efficiency, water use efficiency

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  RCBD, factorial experiment, ANOVA, DMRT/LSD post-hoc test, field trial,
  soil analysis (Kjeldahl, Olsen, flame photometry), randomization, replication
