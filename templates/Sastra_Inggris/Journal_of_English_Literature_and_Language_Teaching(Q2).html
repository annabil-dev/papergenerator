Description

The International Journal of Indo-Pacific Studies, IJIPS (ISSN xxxx-xxxx) is an electronic journal (e-journal) published by Pusat Kajian Indo-Pasifik (Center for Indo-Pacific Studies) of Universitas Negeri Padang (UNP), designed towards fulfilling one of the principal missions of the Pusat Kajian Indo-Pasifik, namely the dissemination of academic research and other scholarly works. With all its inherent advantages, an e-journal serves as the best vehicle to carry IJIPS to the forefront of the global scholarly community.

IJIPS is a scholarly, multidisciplinary, internationally refereed journal focusing primarily on biogeographic region of Indo-Pacific, including Central Indo-Pacific, Eastern Indo-Pacific, Western Indo-Pacific. The disciplines of interest encompass politics, history, indigenous languages and literature, religion, man and the environment, ethno-history, anthropology, cultural heritage, social issues, economic development, war and conflict resolution, prehistory and archaeology, and the arts.

View Journal Current Issue Description

Description

Journal of English Language Teaching is an open-accessed and peer-reviewed journal published by Jurusan Bahasa dan Sastra Inggris, FBS, Universitas Negeri Padang. The journal has the focus and scope on ocuses on the publication of articles that appeal to involved in English Language Teaching (ELT), whether as a second, additional, or foreign language, or as an international Lingua Franca. The Journal links the everyday concerns of practitioners with insights gained from relevant academic disciplines such as applied linguistics, education, and psychology to increase the quality or innovate methodologies in the English Language Teaching. The JELT aims to foster the dissemination of scientific information in the field of English Language Teaching. Articles published in the Journal of English Language Teaching encompass a broad range of research or paper related to teaching English. This journal is published four times a year (March, June, September, and December). The journal provides research and paper to increase awareness, consideration and analysis of issues in Teaching English as Foreign Language and also promotes the interchange of ideas, and comparative studies in this field.

Ecosains: Jurnal Ilmiah Ekonomi dan Pembangunan

View Journal Current Issue Description

Description

Ecosains: Jurnal Ilmiah Ekonomi dan Pembangunan is a peer-reviewed journal publishing empirical research in economics and development. This journal is published twice a year, in May and November, by the Department of Economics, Faculty of Economics, Universitas Negeri Padang.

The Ecosains focuses on a broad range of topics in empirical economics, including monetary economics and banking, international trade, international finance and financial markets, public economics and public finance, industrial organization and market behavior, regional economics and spatial analysis, environmental and natural resource economics, economic growth, institutions and development, human capital, health economics, and demography. Research focusing on the Indonesian economy is prioritized, but regional and global analyses are also welcome.

The journal aims to publish high-quality research that contributes to scientific literature and influences policymaking. Manuscripts from academics, policymakers, and practitioners are welcome regardless of institutional affiliation or geographic location.

The Ecosains was first published in November 2012 in printed form. Since May 2020, Ecosains has been published as a full electronic journal (e-journal).
