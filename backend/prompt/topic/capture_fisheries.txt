EQUATIONS:
- CPUE = C/f (catch per unit effort)
- L(t) = L∞[1 - e^(-K(t-t₀))] (Von Bertalanffy)
- MSY = rK/4 (Schaefer model)
- S(L) = 1/[1 + e^(-b(L-L₅₀))] (selectivity curve)
- F_D = 0.5 × C_D × ρ × A × V² (drag force)
- Z = M + F (total mortality)
- M = e^(0.6543 - 0.279ln(L∞) + 0.6543ln(K) + 0.4634ln(T)) (Pauly's M)
- B = CPUE × q⁻¹ (biomass estimation)
- Y/R = F × e^(-M(tc-tr)) × Σ[Uₙ × e^(-nK(tc-t₀))/(F+M+nK)] (Beverton-Holt)
- W = aL^b (length-weight relationship)
