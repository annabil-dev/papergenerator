Submit a Job Listing

Please fill out the form below to submit a paid employment ad to the St. Louis Review. Employment advertisements are subject to approval and editing. The St. Louis Review charges for employment ads per word. The cost is $10 plus 60 cents per word. An ad that is 100 words will cost $70 per week.

Employment ads run by week, Monday-Monday. To assure inclusion in the following Monday’s paper, the ad must be in by Tuesday at noon (For the Monday, July 15 issue, the ad must be submitted by Tuesday, July 9). If you would like to run your ad for multiple weeks, please select in the field the number of weeks you want the ad printed in the Review.

We do invoices monthly, so the invoice will be sent at the end of the month to the email address you supply.

If you do not see the submission form below, you need to be logged in. Contact stephenkempf@archstl.org.

If you have questions, please email stephenkempf@archstl.org

You must be logged in to post a job.
