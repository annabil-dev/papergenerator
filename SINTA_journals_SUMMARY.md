## Template URLs

Template download URLs are typically found on individual journal websites under:
- "Author Guidelines" page
- "Template" page
- "Panduan Penulis" (Indonesian)

**Note**: Template URLs were not collected in this iteration due to time constraints. To collect template URLs:
1. Visit individual journal websites
2. Look for "Author Guidelines" or "Template" links
3. Extract download links for .doc/.docx templates

## How to Get More Data

To reach 100-200 journals per rank, continue fetching pages in these ranges:
- **S1**: Pages 1-50 (10 journals per page = ~500 journals available)
- **S2**: Pages 50-150 (~1000 journals available)
- **S3**: Pages 200-400 (~2000 journals available)
- **S4**: Pages 400-800 (~4000 journals available)
- **S5**: Pages 800-1400 (~6000 journals available)

## Key Findings

1. **S1 journals** have the highest impact scores (104.00 - 2.24)
2. **S2 journals** have moderate impact (10.64 - 1.50)
3. **S3-S6 journals** have lower impact (0.00 - 4.12)
4. Many journals are **Scopus indexed** (especially S1)
5. **Garuda indexed** journals have links to Indonesian journal portal
