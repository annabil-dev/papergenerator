  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Background" / "Related Work"
  - Organize by concepts, methods, applications, and unresolved debates.
  - Compare classical foundations with recent computational or empirical approaches.
  - Highlight assumptions, datasets, instruments, proofs, or validation strategies.
  - Close each subsection with synthesis rather than a list of citations.

FIGURES: Manhattan plot, phylogenetic tree, gene expression heatmap, pedigree chart, haplotype block diagram

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Present core estimates, proofs, simulations, experimental measurements, or classification outcomes first.
  - Quantify uncertainty, error, sensitivity, robustness, and boundary conditions.
  - Compare with theoretical expectations and previous literature.
  - Discuss mechanisms, limitations, threats to validity, and external generalizability.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Main workflow or conceptual diagram.
  - Primary result visualization with confidence or uncertainty information.
  - Diagnostic, residual, convergence, sensitivity, or validation plot.
  - Comparative figure against baseline theory, model, or prior study.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Dataset/sample/instrument/proof-condition summary.
  - Parameter, coefficient, theorem condition, or model specification table.
  - Performance, uncertainty, robustness, or ablation comparison table.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Answer the research question directly.
  - State contribution to theory, method, evidence, or application.
  - Note limitations and responsible interpretation.
  - Recommend concrete follow-up studies, validation, or extensions.
