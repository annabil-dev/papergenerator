================================================================================
TOPIC GUIDE — Zoology & Entomology
================================================================================
FIELD: Agricultural and Biological Sciences / Biology
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: APA
SUBDISCIPLINE: Zoologi, Entomologi, Morfologi Hewan, Taksonomi Hewan

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. Reports: animal taxon (order, family, species), aspect (morphology,
  behavior, taxonomy, population ecology), location, methods, key findings.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Taxonomic/ecological importance of the group.
  - Gap: poorly documented species, unknown distribution, unstudied life history.
  - Objectives: taxonomy, morphological description, behavioral study, population assessment.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review"
  - Systematic position and taxonomic history.
  - Morphological characters used in identification keys.
  - Previous distributional records in the study region.
  - Behavioral ecology: mating behavior, foraging, life history traits.
  - Pest status (for entomology): crop damage reports, management methods.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Materials and Methods"
  - Collection methods: light traps, malaise traps, sweep nets, pitfall traps, hand collection.
  - Preservation: ethanol 70–95%, pinning, slide mounting for micro-morphology.
  - Morphological examination: stereo microscope (10–40×), compound microscope.
  - Measurements: body length (mm), wingspan (mm), character ratios.
  - Molecular: COI barcoding for species identification.
  - Behavioral study: direct observation with scan sampling or focal animal sampling.
  - Statistical: ANOVA, cluster analysis, discriminant analysis for morphometric data.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Species list with abundance: 20–80 species in diverse habitats.
  - Diversity indices: H' = 2.0–3.5.
  - Morphometric measurements: body length ranges for each species.
  - Behavioral patterns: foraging frequency, activity period.
  Tables: species checklist with abundance, morphometric summary.
  Figures: specimen photos with scale bar, distribution map, diversity bar chart.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Habitus photograph (dorsal/lateral view) with scale bar.
  - Distribution map with occurrence points.
  - Morphometric scatter plot (PCA).
  - Diversity comparison across habitats.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Specimen list: species | location | date | abundance | voucher number.
  - Morphometric data (n, mean ± SD, min–max for each character).
  - Identification key (dichotomous key format).

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - New records, new species descriptions, or taxonomic revisions.
  - Conservation/pest management implications.
  - Limitations: seasonal sampling, detection probability.

COMMON REFERENCE VENUES:
  Zootaxa, Journal of Insect Science, Insect Systematics and Diversity,
  Journal of Natural History, Entomologia Experimentalis et Applicata,
  Systematic Entomology, Journal of Zoological Systematics

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  holotype, paratype, voucher, COI, morphometric, wing venation, genitalia,
  antenna, thorax, abdomen, instar, pupa, imaginal disc, ecdysis, pheromone,
  predation, parasitism, mimicry, cryptic species, barcoding

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  light trapping, malaise trapping, pitfall trapping, COI barcoding,
  morphometric analysis, PCA, dichotomous key, focal animal sampling,
  scan sampling, specimen pinning, slide mounting
