--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Needs analysis (target situation, present situation, learning situation)
   - ESP/EAP methodology (genre analysis, move analysis, corpus-based)
   - Task-based language teaching (TBLT design, implementation, assessment)
   - Content and Language Integrated Learning (CLIL frameworks)
   - Corpus-based materials development (DDL, concordancing)
   - Technology-enhanced language learning (TELL, CALL, MALL, AI-assisted)
   - Pragmatics research (speech acts, politeness, intercultural pragmatics)
   - Genre analysis (Swales move analysis, systemic functional linguistics)
   - Mixed methods research (sequential, convergent, embedded designs)
   - Design-based research (iterative cycles, educational technology)

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics (proficiency scores, needs analysis results, task completion rates, learner satisfaction)
   - All data realistic and internally consistent

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS (where applicable):
   - Corpus linguistics: frequency, TF-IDF, collocational strength (MI score, t-score, log-likelihood)
   - Readability: Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog
   - Inter-rater reliability: Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's alpha
   - Test statistics: item difficulty, discrimination index, reliability (KR-20, Cronbach's alpha)
   - Effect size: Cohen's d, eta-squared, odds ratio

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian/Asian context prioritized
   - Include ESP/EAP scholars (Dudley-Evans, Hyland, Swales) and ASEAN context researchers

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. State the applied linguistics problem, learner/professional context,
  theoretical framework, methodology, principal findings, contribution to applied
  language studies, limitation, and implication for practice.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Define the language use problem in professional/academic/vocational context.
  - Situate within Indonesian multilingual landscape and ASEAN communication needs.
  - Position against current scholarship in applied linguistics and ESP/EAP.
  - State research gap, research questions, objectives, and expected contribution.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Kajian Pustaka" / "Literature Review" / "Theoretical Framework"
  - Core theories in applied linguistics, ESP, and language pedagogy.
  - Recent empirical findings on language for specific purposes in Indonesian context.
  - CEFR framework, TOEFL/IELTS benchmarks, and professional language standards.
  - Gaps in existing research on Indonesian applied foreign language contexts.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Metode Penelitian" / "Research Methods"
  - Design: needs analysis, experimental, action research, design-based, or mixed methods.
  - Participants: Indonesian learners/professionals (institution type, proficiency level).
  - Instruments: needs analysis questionnaires, proficiency tests, task-based assessments, interviews.
  - Analysis: statistical tests, genre/move analysis, thematic coding, corpus analysis.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Present findings by research question with quantitative and qualitative evidence.
  - Compare with prior studies on ESP/EAP in similar contexts.
  - Discuss pedagogical implications, materials design recommendations, and policy suggestions.
  - Tables: needs analysis results, test scores, genre move frequency, learner performance.
  - Figures: needs analysis priority charts, proficiency distribution, task completion rates.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Research design flowchart.
  - Needs analysis priority ranking chart.
  - Genre move structure diagram.
  - Proficiency level distribution.
  - Technology integration framework.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Participant demographics: institution | program | level | L1 | proficiency.
  - Needs analysis: skill/function | importance | frequency | difficulty | priority.
  - Genre analysis: move | step | frequency | obligatory/optional | example.
  - Assessment results: component | pre-test | post-test | gain | effect size.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Answer research questions with specific data-supported claims.
  - State pedagogical, curricular, and policy contributions.
  - Acknowledge limitations in sample, context, and generalizability.
  - Recommend future research with specific populations, settings, or interventions.
