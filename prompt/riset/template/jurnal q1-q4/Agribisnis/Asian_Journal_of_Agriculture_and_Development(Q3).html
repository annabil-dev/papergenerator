Southeast Asian Regional Center for Graduate Study and Research in Agriculture

SEARCA is one of the oldest among 26 specialist institutions of the Southeast Asian Ministers of Education Organization (SEAMEO). Founded on 27 November 1966, SEARCA is mandated to strengthen institutional capacities in agricultural and rural development in Southeast Asia. Learn more.
