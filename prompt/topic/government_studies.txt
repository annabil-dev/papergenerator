--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Qualitative: ethnography, discourse analysis (Fairclough), case study (Yin)
   - Institutional analysis: historical institutionalism, rational choice institutionalism
   - Power analysis: Lukes' three dimensions of power, Gaventa's power cube
   - Governance indicators: World Bank WGI, V-Dem, Transparency International CPI
   - Comparative politics: most similar/most different system design (MSSD/MDSD)
   - Policy network analysis: actor-network mapping, stakeholder analysis
   - Mixed methods: sequential explanatory, embedded design
   - Content analysis: media framing, political discourse
   - Survey research: political participation, trust in government, satisfaction

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics with statistical measures
   - Comparison with baselines
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS (where applicable):
   - Voter turnout rate = (Total votes cast / Registered voters) x 100%
   - Effective Number of Parties (ENP) = 1 / Sum(pi^2) where pi = party vote share
   - Gallagher Disproportionality Index = sqrt(0.5 * Sum(vi - si)^2)
   - Governance Index composite scoring methodology
   - Political participation index calculation
   - Decentralization ratio = Local revenue / Total government revenue
   - Gender Empowerment Measure (GEM) calculation
   - SEM path coefficients for governance model testing

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian governance/political context
   - Reference to pilkada, dana desa, BPD, DPRD, Kemendagri, otonomi daerah

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. Reports: governance phenomenon studied (local politics, electoral dynamics, village governance, political representation), theoretical lens, methodology, unit of analysis, key findings, and implications for democratic governance improvement.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Context: Indonesian democratization, decentralization, local governance reform, electoral democracy
  - Theoretical framework: governance theory (Rhodes, Pierre), power theory (Lukes), democratic theory (Dahl), institutional theory
  - Research gap: understudied local political dynamics, new digital governance, post-reform institutional evolution
  - Research question with clear political/governance variables

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Tinjauan Pustaka" / "Literature Review"
  - Governance theories: good governance, democratic governance, network governance, meta-governance
  - Local government: decentralization, regional autonomy, local politics, political dynasty
  - Electoral politics: electoral system, voter behavior, political participation, campaign strategy
  - Village governance: village democracy, BPD, musyawarah, village fund governance
  - Political parties: party system, party institutionalization, cadre recruitment, coalition
  - Indonesian context: pilkada, DPRD, Kemendagri, otonomi daerah, reformasi

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Metode Penelitian" / "Research Methodology"
  - Qualitative: ethnography, case study, discourse analysis, phenomenology, grounded theory
  - Quantitative: survey, regression analysis, content analysis (quantitative)
  - Comparative: MSSD/MDSD, cross-case analysis, QCA (Qualitative Comparative Analysis)
  - Data sources: government documents, election data (KPU), media archives, interviews, observation
  - Sampling: purposive, snowball (qualitative), random/stratified (quantitative)
  - Analysis: thematic analysis, discourse analysis, network analysis, NVivo, ATLAS.ti

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Electoral data: voter turnout (60-80%), vote share distribution, ENP (3-8)
  - Governance indicators: WGI scores (-2.5 to +2.5), local governance index
  - Political participation: participation rate, engagement level, trust scores
  - Policy implementation: compliance rate, effectiveness indicators
  - Power dynamics: actor mapping, influence networks, coalition patterns
  Tables: election results, governance indicators, actor-network matrices
  Figures: power maps, network diagrams, electoral trend charts, governance radar

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  governance, decentralization, regional autonomy, local politics, electoral democracy,
  political participation, representation, accountability, transparency,
  village governance, political dynasty, patronage, clientelism,
  democratic consolidation, political party, coalition, opposition,
  pilkada, DPRD, BPD, musyawarah, otonomi daerah

INDONESIAN CONTEXT:
  - Pilkada: simultaneous regional elections, direct election of regional heads
  - Dana Desa: village fund governance, BPD oversight, village deliberation
  - DPRD: regional parliament, legislative function, oversight function, budgeting
  - Kemendagri: Ministry of Home Affairs, regional governance supervision
  - Otonomi Daerah: UU No. 23/2014, transfer of authority, asymmetric decentralization
  - Political Dynasty: family politics, incumbent advantage, democratic quality
  - Village Governance: UU No. 6/2014, village head election, BPD, village apparatus
  - Electoral System: proportional representation, electoral threshold, campaign regulation
