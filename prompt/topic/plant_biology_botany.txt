================================================================================
TOPIC GUIDE — Plant Biology & Botany
================================================================================
FIELD: Agricultural and Biological Sciences / Biology
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: APA
SUBDISCIPLINE: Botani, Fisiologi Tumbuhan, Anatomi Tumbuhan, Taksonomi Tumbuhan, Etnobotani

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. Reports: plant species (genus, species, family), aspect studied
  (anatomy, physiology, taxonomy, phytochemistry), methods, key findings
  (morphological characters, physiological parameters, bioactive compounds).

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Ecological/economic importance of the study species or plant group.
  - Taxonomic uncertainty or phytochemical potential.
  - Research gap: unstudied species, undescribed variety, unknown bioactive profile.
  - Objectives: morphological characterization, phylogenetic placement, phytochemical screening.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Background"
  - Systematic position and taxonomic history.
  - Related species phytochemistry/bioactivity.
  - Phytochemical classes: alkaloids, flavonoids, terpenoids, saponins, tannins.
  - Plant anatomy: cell types, tissue organization, modifications.
  - Physiological processes relevant to study: photosynthesis, transpiration, stomatal regulation.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Materials and Methods"
  - Specimen collection: location, date, voucher herbarium number.
  - Morphological study: morphometric measurements (leaf length × width mm, petal number).
  - Anatomical study: cross-sections (hand-cut or microtome 10–15 µm), staining (safranin-fast green).
  - Phytochemical screening: qualitative tests (alkaloid-Dragendorff, flavonoid-AlCl3, tannin-FeCl3).
  - Quantitative phytochemistry: spectrophotometry, HPLC for active compound quantification.
  - Molecular phylogeny: DNA extraction, PCR (rbcL, matK, ITS), sequence alignment, phylogenetic tree.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Morphology: 15–30 morphological characters measured and described.
  - Anatomy: cell dimensions (parenchyma diameter 20–80 µm), vascular bundle counts.
  - Phytochemical: total flavonoid 10–50 mg QE/g DW; total phenolic 50–200 mg GAE/g DW.
  - HPLC: identification of quercetin, kaempferol, chlorogenic acid with retention times.
  - Phylogeny: bootstrap values >70% for supported clades.
  Tables: morphometric data, phytochemical compound table, phylogenetic distance matrix.
  Figures: herbarium specimen photo, anatomical cross-section micrograph, HPLC chromatogram, phylogenetic tree.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Herbarium specimen photograph with scale bar.
  - Anatomical cross-section micrograph (light microscopy, 100×–400×).
  - HPLC chromatogram with compound identification.
  - Phylogenetic tree (Neighbor-Joining or Maximum Likelihood).
  - SEM image (pollen or trichome morphology).

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Morphometric data (mean ± SD for quantitative characters).
  - Phytochemical screening results (compound class | reaction | result +/-).
  - Quantitative phytochemistry by plant part (leaf, stem, root).

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Summarizes morphological/phytochemical/taxonomic findings.
  - Taxonomic conclusion: confirmed species identity or new record.
  - Pharmaceutical/ethnobotanical potential based on findings.
  - Limitations: single population, seasonal variation.

COMMON REFERENCE VENUES:
  Plant Systematics and Evolution, Phytochemistry, Plant Biology,
  Journal of Plant Research, Botanical Journal of the Linnean Society,
  Flora (Morphology, Distribution, Functional Ecology of Plants), PLOS ONE

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:

  flavonoid, terpenoid, saponin, tannin, phenolic, HPLC, spectrophotometry,
  bootstrap, clade, parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma, stomata, trichome

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:

  HPLC analysis, DNA barcoding, PCR, phylogenetic analysis (MEGA), SEM imaging
