Submission Preparation Checklist

As part of the submission process, authors are required to check off their submission's compliance with all of the following items, and submissions may be returned to authors that do not adhere to these guidelines.

  • I/we have read and agree to the Privacy Policy of this journal.
  • I/we have prepared a PDF manuscript in JSS style.
  • I/we have prepared the source code for the software.
  • I/we have prepared the replication materials for all results from the manuscript, preferably a standalone replication script.
  • The license(s) of the software is clearly indicated (includes the GPL-2 or GPL-3 or a GPL-compatible license for publication in JSS).

Author Guidelines

The Author Guidelines are included in the Information For Authors page where once can also find information on the journal's Style Guide and a step-by-step Submission guide.

Support

As a matter of principle, JSS charges no author fees or subscription fees. Our editors, reviewers, and programmers are volunteers. Universität Innsbruck, Universität Zürich, and UCLA Statistics provide support staff, website maintenance, website hosting, and some graduate student support. Because of our success and growth we do need more resources in the future. You can support us by becoming a member of the Foundation for Open Access Statistics at www.foastat.org, and by contributing on their donation page.
